# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- Collect and organize callouts across your vault.
- Filter by callout type, folder, or search text.
- Click results to jump to their source.
- Configure custom callout colors, borders, and icons.
- Includes presets for `todo`, `idea`, `note`, `hook`, `rule`, and `clue`.
- Provides autocomplete suggestions in `callout-tracker` blocks.
